Resolution of Fisher-KPP type reaction diffusion equations through learning-based methods

Research Project · École Polytechnique, Paris

Under the supervision of Ziad Kobeissi, I implemented Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) in PyTorch to solve nonlinear reaction-diffusion equations like Fisher-KPP. By coding and benchmarking both residual-based and energy-based weak formulations, I analyzed the optimization bottlenecks caused by their non-convex loss landscapes. The core empirical finding is that increasing MLP depth acts primarily as an optimization mechanism, driving down PDE residuals faster, rather than simply providing extra representational capacity.
